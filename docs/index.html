Senku Cuts

A desktop tool for preparing short vertical clips from my own gameplay recordings.

What it does

Senku Cuts runs locally on the owner's computer. It takes a long gameplay recording, transcribes the narration, splits the recording into parts at natural sentence boundaries, and renders vertical 1080×1920 clips with burned-in subtitles.

The finished clips are uploaded to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. Nothing is published automatically — the owner reviews each draft inside the TikTok app and decides whether to post it.

Who it is for

This is a single-user tool built for one creator to manage their own content. It does not offer accounts, sign-ups, or services to other people.

TikTok permissions used

  • user.info.basic — to confirm which account the clips are being uploaded to.
  • video.upload — to place finished clips into the account's drafts.

The application does not publish content, does not read other users' data, and does not access anything beyond the account that authorized it.
